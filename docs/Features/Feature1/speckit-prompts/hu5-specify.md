Build an email notification feature for the waitlist system. When a buyer's waitlist opportunity becomes active, the system sends an email to the buyer's registered email address. The email informs the buyer that they have an active purchase opportunity, that their ticket is temporarily reserved for 15 minutes, references the event name, states the validity period, and instructs the buyer to check their status within the application. The email is explicitly an informational alert — it is not the official source of state. If the email provider returns an error, the system logs the failed attempt but the opportunity remains active regardless. Every send attempt (successful or failed) is audited in a notification_deliveries table with the result and timestamp. The email channel is isolated from the assignment logic: a failure in email delivery never affects the opportunity lifecycle. The trigger for sending is the activation of the opportunity, not any buyer action.
